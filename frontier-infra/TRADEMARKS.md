Frontier Infra names, logos, and conformance labels identify the Frontier Infra
standards and plugin package. Open-source copyright licenses do not grant
separate trademark, certification, or endorsement rights.

Use product and provider names such as Codex, OpenAI, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini,
or Grok only for accurate nominative reference. Do not imply that this plugin
is authored, sponsored, certified, or endorsed by those providers unless a
separate written authorization exists.

Conformance labels such as `Machine-L3`, `Orchestrator-L4`, `AVL conformant`,
or `AAR receipted` should be used only with current evidence from the relevant
specification, tests, and dated artifacts. A logo, badge, README statement, or
architecture resemblance is not a conformance grant.
